# Covers

The cover images of the blog posts and the tutorials. The style and the steps
to add a cover are in ["Cover images" in `AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md#cover-images).

- `style.mjs`: the style (background, colors, logos) and the drawing helpers.
- `covers.mjs`: one entry per cover.
- `render.mjs`: renders the covers to JPEG with Google Chrome.
- `icons/`: line icons from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC license), plus `bucket.svg`, drawn for these covers.
- `logos/`: brand marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0 license). The brands are trademarks of their owners.
