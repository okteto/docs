## When to use StatefulSets

StatefulSets become useful in applications that require things like:

- Stable, unique network identifiers.
- Stable, persistent storage.
- Ordered, graceful deployment and scaling.
- Ordered, automated rolling updates. [from kubernetes.io](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/controllers/statefulset/)

If you’re using Okteto and want to make use of StatefulSets, you won’t want to miss Abdul's demonstration at the next Community Call.
