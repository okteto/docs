## What are secrets? 

When you’re developing an application you’ll often have confidential information or tokens that you don’t want to leave exposed. Normally, you’ll save this information locally, but what happens when you want to use Okteto and you’re concerned about how you will store these secrets? 

That’s where Okteto’s secrets management can help. 

For more information on secrets, [check out our docs](https://okteto.com/docs/cloud/secrets/#manage-okteto-secrets-from-the-okteto-cloud-ui).
