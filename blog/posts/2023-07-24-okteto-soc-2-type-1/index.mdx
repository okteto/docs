We’re excited to announce that Okteto has successfully passed a third-party audit and received a SOC 2® Type I report. This achievement is more than just an accolade; it's a testament to our unwavering commitment to safeguarding our customers' data.

{/* truncate */}

We completed an independent audit, with the support of Strike Graph, confirming Okteto's controls related to our information security practices, policies, procedures and operations met the rigorous SOC 2 standards for security, availability, processing, integrity, confidentiality, and privacy as developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA).

## What Does SOC 2 Compliance Mean for You?

SOC 2 is a technical audit designed to ensure that service providers securely manage your data to protect the interests of your organization and the privacy of your customers. By achieving this compliance, we have demonstrated that we have reliable and effective controls in place to safeguard your sensitive data.

In addition, SOC 2 compliance gives you confidence that Okteto is dependable and secure. It means that our solutions are built with security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access and use. Moreover, it demonstrates that Okteto takes data security seriously and is dedicated to keeping customers' trust and loyalty.

## Your Trust, Our Priority

At Okteto, we understand the trust you place in us when you entrust us with your critical data. We value your security and prioritize your privacy above all else. We thank you for choosing Okteto and assure you that we will continue to serve you with the highest standards of security and trust.

"At Okteto, we are deeply committed to providing a secure and reliable platform for our customers," said Ramiro Berrelleza, CEO of Okteto. We're proud to have successfully achieved this compliance and will continue our commitment to providing a secure platform and processes for our customers. With SOC 2 compliance, you can trust that Okteto is the right choice for your cloud native development needs."

While we take a moment to celebrate this achievement, we understand that maintaining and enhancing our security posture is a continuous journey in guiding our customers to [building world-class development experiences](https://www.okteto.com/build-development-experiences/) for their organizations. We will continue to invest in robust security measures and prioritize transparency in our processes.

Thank you for being a part of our journey. Here's to many more milestones together!
