On July 22nd at 10am Pacific/1pm Eastern we’ll be hosting our third Community Call and you’re invited to join us! This month, we’ll be meeting on Zoom and YouTube, and we’d like to have members of our community participate with questions or any cool Okteto use cases you’d like to share. 

<!-- truncate -->

[Register now](https://lu.ma/qgm1qff3)

<!-- more -->

This month, we’ll be hearing from some members of the team at [Octobot](https://github.com/Drakkar-Software/OctoBot). [Guillaume De Saint Martin](https://github.com/GuillaumeDSM), and [Herklos](https://github.com/Herklos) will talk about their experience using Okteto with Octobot, plus their recent experiments with preview environments.  

Also, our Developer Advocate [Abdulazeez Abdulazeez](https://twitter.com/kvng_zeez), will be giving a sneak peak walkthrough of our new preview environments UI. Get a first look at the updated UI and the dedicated CLI command that are coming soon! 

Our community calls are for you—our community! We want to hear your voices, so come prepared to chat if you’d like to, or feel free to just sit back and watch. 

Come meet your fellow Okteto users, learn what’s new, and get your questions answered!

[Register Here](https://lu.ma/qgm1qff3)

See you there!
