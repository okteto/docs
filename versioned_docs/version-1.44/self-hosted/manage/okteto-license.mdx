An Okteto license key unlocks the Okteto platform. [Billing](admin/billing.mdx) explains how billing works, what your license includes, how seats are counted, and how true-ups are handled.

## When a license is required

Okteto requires a license key to use any of our paid products: [BYOC and Self-Hosted](saas-vs-self-hosted.mdx). The license is what unlocks the platform, but it does not by itself determine your bill. Your seat count, allowance, and any overages are governed by your Order Form and our [billing model](admin/billing.mdx).

## Obtaining a license

You can get a license in two ways:

1. Fill out the self-hosted [Free Tier form](https://www.okteto.com/free-trial/) on our website.
1. [Contact us](https://www.okteto.com/get-demo/) to schedule a demo.

When you fill out the self-hosted Free Tier form, you will automatically receive an email containing a license key for your Free Tier access. This option does not require any interaction with Okteto or our teams and gives you the control and independence to try Okteto Self-Hosted on your schedule and at your own pace. The Free Tier license only works with Self-Hosted.

When you contact us for a demo, someone from the Okteto team gives you a personalized demo, answers your questions, and explores the product with you. This process can result in a Free Tier license for Okteto Self-Hosted.

You don't need a credit card to start.

## Installing the license

### Bring Your Own Cloud

No action is required: Okteto manages the license for BYOC instances.

### Self-Hosted

Add your license key to your `config.yaml` with the `license` key. For example:

```yaml
license: ABC123...XYZ456
```

:::note
You can also provide your Okteto license through [Cloud Secrets](self-hosted/helm-configuration.mdx#secret).
:::

After you update the license, [upgrade your Okteto instance](self-hosted/manage/upgrade.mdx) for the new license to take effect.

## Checking your seat usage

The [Admin Dashboard](admin/dashboard.mdx) and the [Okteto API](admin/okteto-api.mdx) show your current account count. [Billing](admin/billing.mdx) explains what counts as a seat and how overages are handled.

## License expiration

When a paid license expires, your instance keeps running, but you can't create new accounts until you renew the license. Free Tier and test licenses block all logins when they expire. Contact your account team well before your renewal date.
