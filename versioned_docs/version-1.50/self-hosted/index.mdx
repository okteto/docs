Okteto Self-Hosted runs Okteto on your own infrastructure.

You install the Okteto platform in your private cloud or on-premises infrastructure, and you keep full control over its configuration and security.

Okteto Self-Hosted is distributed as a [Helm chart](https://artifacthub.io/packages/helm/okteto/okteto) that contains every Okteto component:

- NGINX Ingress
- Okteto/API
- Okteto/BuildKit
- Okteto/Frontend
- Okteto/Migration
- Okteto/Registry
- Okteto/Validation Webhook
