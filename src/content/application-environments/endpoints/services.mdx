Okteto creates an HTTPS endpoint for any Service with the `dev.okteto.com/auto-ingress: "true"` annotation:

```yaml
apiVersion: v1
kind: Service
metadata:
  name: hello-world
  labels:
    app: hello-world
  annotations:
    dev.okteto.com/auto-ingress: "true"
spec:
  type: ClusterIP
  ports:
    - port: 8080
      protocol: TCP
      targetPort: 8080
  selector:
    app: hello-world
```

The endpoint forwards traffic to the first HTTP port of your Service, and its hostname is `<service-name>-<namespace>.<okteto-subdomain>`. The Okteto UI and `okteto endpoints` show its URL.

## Ingress or HTTPRoute

To expose the Service, Okteto creates an [Ingress](application-environments/endpoints/ingress.mdx) or an [HTTPRoute](application-environments/endpoints/httproutes.mdx) for you, depending on the Helm value [`unsupported.defaultGateway.type`](self-hosted/manage/migrate-to-gateway-api.mdx#docker-compose-endpoints) of your Okteto instance: `ingress` (default) or `gateway`. You don't need to change your Service when your Okteto instance moves from one to the other.

## LoadBalancer and NodePort Services

Okteto manages `NodePort` and `LoadBalancer` Services as if they had the `dev.okteto.com/auto-ingress: "true"` annotation: it converts them to `ClusterIP` and creates an endpoint for them. You can disable this behavior by setting [`convertLoadBalancedServices.enabled`](self-hosted/helm-configuration.mdx#convertloadbalancedservices) to `false`.
