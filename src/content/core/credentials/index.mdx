Credentials let you, your tools, and your automations authenticate with Okteto outside the Okteto UI:

- [Kubernetes Credentials](core/credentials/kubernetes-credentials.mdx): a kubeconfig for your Okteto Namespaces, so `kubectl`, Helm, and your other tools work with them.
- [Personal Access Tokens](core/credentials/personal-access-tokens.mdx): tokens that authenticate the Okteto CLI and calls to the Okteto API as you, without a browser.
- [Environment Variables](core/credentials/environment-variables.mdx): the variables, such as `OKTETO_TOKEN`, that pass credentials to the Okteto CLI in scripts and CI pipelines.

For shared automations, such as the CI pipelines that deploy Preview Environments, an Admin creates [Admin Access Tokens](admin/admin-access-tokens.mdx) instead of Personal Access Tokens.
