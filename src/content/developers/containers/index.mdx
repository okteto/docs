Development Containers let you develop your application in Kubernetes with hot reload and a debugger. When you run [`okteto up`](reference/okteto-cli.mdx#up), [File Sync](developers/file-synchronization.mdx) keeps your local code synchronized with the Development Container running in your cluster.

In this example, `okteto up` replaces the **api** Kubernetes Deployment with a development copy, a Deployment called **api-okteto**, with these changes:

- Okteto overrides the container configuration of **api-okteto** with the values of your [Development Container](reference/okteto-manifest.mdx#dev-object-optional). For example, it replaces the production image with one that has your development tools.
- A [file synchronization service](developers/file-synchronization.mdx) keeps your local filesystem and your Development Container in sync, in both directions.
- Okteto forwards ports over [SSH](developers/ssh-server.mdx), so you can reach the services of your cluster on `localhost`.

Your Development Container inherits the manifest of the original **api** Deployment, so it uses the same service account, environment variables, secrets, volumes, and sidecars. You develop in the same conditions your application runs in production.
