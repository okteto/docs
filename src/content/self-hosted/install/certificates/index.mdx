# Configure the Okteto Wildcard Certificate

By default, Okteto creates a self-signed wildcard certificate for your cluster. It secures the communication with the Okteto UI, the Okteto Build Service, the Okteto Registry, and every application exposed through [Endpoints](application-environments/endpoints/automatic-ssl.mdx).

In production, use your own certificate instead of the self-signed one. You can configure your wildcard certificate with any of the following approaches:

- [cert-manager and Let's Encrypt](self-hosted/install/certificates/cert-manager.mdx)
- [AWS Certificate Manager](self-hosted/install/certificates/aws-acm.mdx)
- [Bring your own certificate](self-hosted/install/certificates/bring-your-own-certificate.mdx)

If you can't use wildcard certificates, the Okteto community maintains a guide on [how to install Okteto without a wildcard certificate](https://community.okteto.com/t/how-do-i-install-okteto-without-a-wildcard-certificate/796).
