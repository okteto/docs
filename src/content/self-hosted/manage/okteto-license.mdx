An Okteto license key unlocks the Okteto platform. [Billing](admin/billing.mdx) explains how billing works, what your license includes, how seats are counted, and how true-ups are handled.

## When a license is required

Okteto requires a license key for its paid products: [Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC) and Self-Hosted](byoc-vs-self-hosted.mdx). The license unlocks the platform, but it doesn't determine your bill on its own: your Order Form and the [billing model](admin/billing.mdx) govern your seat count, allowance, and any overages.

## Obtaining a license

You can get a license in two ways:

1. Fill out the Self-Hosted [Free Tier form](https://www.okteto.com/free-tier/) on the Okteto website.
1. [Contact us](https://www.okteto.com/get-demo/) to schedule a demo.

When you fill out the Self-Hosted Free Tier form, you automatically receive an email with a Free Tier license key. You don't need to talk to anyone at Okteto, so you can try Okteto Self-Hosted on your own schedule. The Free Tier license is valid for one year, covers up to 5 users and 500 environments per month, and only works with Self-Hosted.

When you contact us for a demo, someone from the Okteto team gives you a personalized demo, answers your questions, and explores the product with you. This process can result in Free Tier access to either Okteto BYOC or Self-Hosted.

You don't need a credit card to start.

## Installing the license

### Bring Your Own Cloud

No action is required: Okteto manages the license for BYOC instances.

### Self-Hosted

Add your license key to your `config.yaml` with the `license` key. For example:

```yaml
license: ABC123...XYZ456
```

:::note
You can also provide your Okteto license through [Cloud Secrets](self-hosted/helm-configuration.mdx#secret).
:::

After you update the license, [upgrade your Okteto instance](self-hosted/manage/upgrade.mdx) for the new license to take effect.

## Checking your seat usage

The [Admin Dashboard](admin/dashboard.mdx) and the [Okteto API](admin/okteto-api.mdx) show your current account count. [Billing](admin/billing.mdx) explains what counts as a seat and how overages are handled.

## License expiration

When a paid license expires, your instance keeps running, but you can't create new accounts until you renew the license. Free Tier and trial licenses block all logins when they expire. Contact your account team well before your renewal date.
