An Okteto license key unlocks the platform, and Okteto bills per seat on an annual contract.

## License requirements

Okteto's paid products, [Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC) and Self-Hosted](byoc-vs-self-hosted.mdx), require a license key. The license unlocks the platform, but it doesn't set your bill on its own: your Order Form and the [billing model](#billing) govern your seat count, allowance, and any overages.

## Obtaining a license

You can get a license in two ways:

1. Fill out the Self-Hosted [Free Tier form](https://www.okteto.com/free-tier/) on the Okteto website.
1. [Contact us](https://www.okteto.com/get-demo/) to schedule a demo.

When you fill out the Self-Hosted Free Tier form, you get an email with a Free Tier license key right away. You don't need to talk to anyone at Okteto, so you can try Okteto Self-Hosted on your own schedule. The Free Tier license is valid for one year, covers up to 5 users and 500 environments per month, and only works with Self-Hosted.

When you contact us for a demo, someone from the Okteto team gives you a personalized demo, answers your questions, and explores the product with you. A demo can also lead to a Free Tier license for Okteto Self-Hosted.

You don't need a credit card to start.

## Installing the license

### Bring Your Own Cloud

Okteto manages the license for BYOC instances, so you don't need to do anything.

### Self-Hosted

Add your license key to your `config.yaml` with the `license` key. For example:

```yaml
license: ABC123...XYZ456
```

:::note
You can also provide your Okteto license through [Cloud Secrets](self-hosted/helm-configuration.mdx#secret).
:::

After you update the license, [upgrade your Okteto instance](self-hosted/manage/upgrade.mdx) so the new license takes effect.

## License expiration

When a paid license expires, your instance keeps running, but you can't create new accounts until you renew the license. Free Tier and test licenses block all logins when they expire. Contact your account team well before your renewal date.

## Billing

- **Pricing is per seat**, billed annually.
- **No charge per agent or per environment.** Each developer can run as many application environments and agents as your infrastructure supports.
- **Enterprise license covers your entire fleet.** A single license lets you deploy as many Okteto clusters as you need, and we only charge for unique seats across all of them.
- **No hard cap.** Okteto doesn't block new user accounts or interrupt service, and a true-up settles any overage.
- **Self-service visibility.** See your seat count at any time in the [Admin Dashboard](admin/users.mdx) or with the [Okteto API](reference/okteto-api.mdx).

### Seats

A seat is one user account in your Okteto instance, identified by an email address. When a user logs in for the first time, Okteto creates their account automatically.

Okteto does **not** count:

- The number of application environments a developer creates.
- The number of agents (Okteto, AI, or otherwise) that operate on behalf of that developer.
- The service accounts and bots that the platform itself uses.

If you bought 50 seats, you can have 50 accounts in your system. Each one can run as many environments and agents as your cluster supports.

:::note
Your contract says "Users" and "User Allowance", and this page says "seat". The terms are equivalent.
:::

### Enterprise multi-cluster licensing

For Enterprise customers, your Okteto license covers your entire fleet. You can deploy Okteto on as many clusters as you need (production, staging, regional, per-team) at no extra cost.

Billing counts **unique seats across the fleet**: a user with the same email in two clusters counts once. You can split workloads, isolate environments, or run regional clusters without paying twice for the same developer.

### Managing your seat count

- **Delete user accounts you no longer need.** A deleted account leaves the count immediately. You can't reassign an account to another person: when a different person logs in with their email, Okteto creates a new account automatically.
- **Use Okta to deprovision automatically.** If your team uses Okta, configure [Okta User Deprovisioning](admin/general-integrations.mdx#okta-user-deprovisioning) to delete accounts automatically when users are removed from your identity provider.
- **Use the Okteto API.** The [Okteto API](reference/okteto-api.mdx) lists users and deletes them by ID, so you can add account cleanup to your own offboarding scripts or integrations.

For permanent team growth, contact your Okteto account team to purchase additional seats in advance. Add-on seats are typically cheaper than waiting for a true-up.

### Seat usage

The [Admin Dashboard](admin/users.mdx) and the [Okteto API](reference/okteto-api.mdx) show your current account count.

Okteto checks your account count every month. If it exceeds your allowance, your Okteto account team contacts you.

### Seat overages

Okteto doesn't block new accounts when you exceed your seat allowance.

When the count of user accounts exceeds your purchased seat allowance:

1. **Okteto notifies you before invoicing.** Unless you've asked otherwise, Okteto contacts you before it invoices any true-up. Add-on seats purchased proactively are typically cheaper than waiting for the true-up.
2. **True-up at your contracted rate.** The additional user seats are billed at the per-seat price on your Order Form, pro-rated over the remaining months of your current annual term, and invoiced immediately.
3. **Renewal updated.** At the start of your next annual period, your subscription is updated to reflect the new seat count.

## FAQ

### Do I get charged when a developer runs an extra environment?

No. Okteto charges for seats, not environments. A developer with one seat can run as many environments as your cluster supports.

### What about AI agents or build agents that use Okteto on a developer's behalf?

They aren't counted. A seat is a person, identified by email.

### Can I reassign a seat from a former employee to their replacement?

No. Delete the former employee's account. When their replacement logs in with their own email, Okteto creates a new account automatically.

### Our team grew permanently. We have 10 extra accounts in the system. What happens?

A true-up covers them. The 10 additional seats are billed at your contracted rate, pro-rated over the remaining months of your annual term, and your renewal reflects the new seat count.

### A developer left. Do I deactivate the account or delete it?

Delete it. Deactivation doesn't remove the account from the count.

### Is there a hard cap?

No. Okteto doesn't block new accounts. The platform stays fully usable, and a true-up settles the difference at invoicing.

### If we run multiple Okteto clusters, do we pay for each cluster?

No. For Enterprise, a single license covers your entire fleet. We count unique seats across all your clusters: a developer with the same email in two clusters counts once.
