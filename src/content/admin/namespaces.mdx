**Admin > Namespaces** lists every [Namespace](core/namespaces.mdx) of your Okteto instance, with its owner, its status (active or sleeping), and when it was last updated. Filter the list by owner, status, or date, or search it by name.

<Image
  src={require("@site/static/img/admin-namespaces.png").default}
  alt="The Namespaces section of the Admin Dashboard, with filters by owner, status, and date, and a table of Namespaces with their owner, status, and last update"
/>

From the menu at the end of each row, an Admin can:

- Open the Namespace, with read-only access, by clicking its name.
- Wake a sleeping Namespace.
- Mark the Namespace as persistent, so the [Garbage Collector](admin/cleanup.mdx) never sleeps or deletes it.
- [Transfer the Namespace](core/namespaces.mdx#transfer-namespace-ownership) to another owner.

The last update is the most recent activity in the Namespace, such as a deployment or an `okteto up` session. [What counts as activity](admin/cleanup.mdx#what-counts-as-activity) lists every kind.
