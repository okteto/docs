**Admin > Namespaces** lists every [Namespace](application-environments/namespaces.mdx) of your Okteto instance, with its owner, its status (active or sleeping), and when it was last updated.

## Filtering and Searching Namespaces

These filters narrow down the list of Namespaces:

- **Search**: finds Namespaces by name.
- **Owner**: filters by the user who owns the Namespace. You can search the list of users.
- **Status**: filters by status, such as active or sleeping.
- **Updated**: filters by the time of the last update:
  - Last Hour
  - Last 24 Hours
  - Last 7 Days
  - Last 30 Days
  - Last 90 Days

You can combine several filters.

## Namespace Table Columns

The table shows these columns for each Namespace:

- **Name**: the Namespace's name. Click it to open the Namespace with read-only access.
- **Owner**: the user who owns the Namespace.
- **Status**: the Namespace's current status, for example active or sleeping.
- **Last Updated**: the most recent activity in the Namespace, such as a deployment or an `okteto up` session. [Activity](admin/cleanup.mdx#what-counts-as-activity) lists every kind.

## Managing Namespaces

The menu at the end of each row has these actions:

- **Transfer Ownership**: makes another user the owner of the Namespace. Select **Keep current owner as a member** to keep the current owner's access. Personal Namespaces can't be transferred. [Transferring ownership](application-environments/namespaces.mdx#transfer-namespace-ownership) has the details.
- **Wake Up**: wakes up a sleeping Namespace.
- **Put To Sleep**: puts an active Namespace to sleep to save resources. It's disabled for persistent Namespaces.
- **Persistent**: marks the Namespace as persistent, so it's never deleted or put to sleep for inactivity. The [Garbage Collector](admin/cleanup.mdx) doesn't delete it either, because it stays active.

:::tip
A persistent Namespace stays available until someone deletes it. Mark shared Namespaces that must always be reachable, such as a staging environment, as **Persistent**.
:::
