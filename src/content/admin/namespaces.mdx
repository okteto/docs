**Admin > Namespaces** lists every [Namespace](application-environments/namespaces.mdx) of your Okteto instance, with its owner, its status (active or sleeping), and when it was last updated.

## Filtering and Searching Namespaces

These filters narrow down the list of Namespaces:

- **Search**: finds Namespaces by name.
- **Owner**: filters by the user who owns the Namespace. You can search the list of users.
- **Status**: filters by status, such as active or sleeping.
- **Updated**: filters by the time of the last update:
  - Last Hour
  - Last 24 Hours
  - Last 7 Days
  - Last 30 Days
  - Last 90 Days

You can combine several filters.

## Namespace Table Columns

The table shows these columns for each Namespace:

- **Name**: the Namespace's name. Click it to open the Namespace with read-only access.
- **Owner**: the user who owns the Namespace.
- **Status**: the Namespace's current status, for example active or sleeping.
- **Last Updated**: the most recent activity in the Namespace, such as a deployment or an `okteto up` session. [Activity](admin/cleanup.mdx#what-counts-as-activity) lists every kind.

## Managing Namespaces

The menu at the end of each row has these actions:

- **Transfer Ownership**: makes another user the owner of the Namespace. See [Transferring Ownership](#transfer-namespace-ownership).
- **Wake Up**: wakes up a sleeping Namespace.
- **Put To Sleep**: puts an active Namespace to sleep to save resources. It's disabled for persistent Namespaces.
- **Persistent**: marks the Namespace as persistent. See [Persistent Namespaces](#persistent-namespaces).

Developers manage their own Namespaces, and the ones shared with them. [User Roles and Permissions](admin/user-roles-and-permissions.mdx) lists what each role can do, and how to [customize the role of Developers](admin/user-roles-and-permissions.mdx#customizing-namespace-roles) in their Namespaces.

### Transferring Ownership {#transfer-namespace-ownership}

**Transfer Ownership** makes another user the owner of a Namespace. Select **Keep current owner as a member** to keep the current owner's access. Personal Namespaces can't be transferred.

The current owner's [user variables](user-settings/variables.mdx) don't move with the Namespace. Add the variables that its application environments need for the new owner, or their next deploy or destroy can fail.

### Persistent Namespaces {#persistent-namespaces}

**Persistent** marks a Namespace as persistent: the [Garbage Collector](admin/cleanup.mdx) never puts it to sleep or deletes it, and nobody can delete it from the Okteto UI. Mark the Namespaces that must always be available as persistent, such as a staging environment or the [Shared Namespace for Divert](application-environments/divert/shared-namespace.mdx).
