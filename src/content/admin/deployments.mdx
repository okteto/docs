**Admin > Deployments** sets the default behavior for applications with [dependencies](application-environments/okteto-manifest.mdx#dependencies):

- **Redeploy Including Dependencies:** redeploying an application also redeploys its direct dependencies.
- **Destroy Including Dependencies:** destroying an application also destroys its direct dependencies.
