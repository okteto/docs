**Admin > Overview** shows the state of your Okteto instance at a glance:

- **License:** your plan and the date your license expires.
- **Users:** the number of accounts in your instance, which is what counts as seats. [Licensing and Billing](self-hosted/manage/okteto-license.mdx#seats) explains how seats work.
- **Chart Version**, **CLI Version**, and **Kubernetes Version:** the versions of the Okteto Helm chart, of the Okteto CLI that your instance ships, and of your Kubernetes cluster.
- **Nodes**, **Namespaces**, and **Previews:** how many there are, with a **View** button that opens [Nodes](admin/nodes.mdx), [Namespaces](admin/namespaces.mdx), and [Previews](admin/previews.mdx).

The Okteto Helm chart version is also available to every user in the **Help** menu, so anyone can report it without Admin access.
