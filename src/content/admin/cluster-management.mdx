**Cluster Management** groups the sections of the Admin Dashboard that show the state of your Okteto instance: its license and versions, its users, its nodes, its Namespaces, and its Preview Environments.

- [Overview](admin/overview.mdx): your license, the versions of Okteto and Kubernetes, and the size of your instance.
- [Installation](admin/installation.mdx): the tasks that complete the setup of your instance.
- [Catalog](admin/catalog.mdx): ready-to-deploy applications for your developers.
- [Users](admin/users.mdx): the accounts of your instance, their roles, and when they were last active.
- [Build Service](admin/build-service.mdx): the performance of the Okteto Build Service and the thresholds that route builds.
- [Nodes](admin/nodes.mdx): the usage of each node of your cluster.
- [Namespaces](admin/namespaces.mdx): every Namespace of your instance, to wake, persist, or transfer them.
- [Previews](admin/previews.mdx): every Preview Environment of your instance.
