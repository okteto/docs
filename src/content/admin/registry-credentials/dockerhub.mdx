Okteto pulls images from your private Docker Hub repositories with the [Registry Credentials](admin/registry-credentials/index.mdx) you configure in the Admin Dashboard. Docker Hub credentials also raise Docker Hub's pull rate limits, as [Pull Rate Limits](admin/registry-credentials/index.mdx#pull-rate-limits) explains.

Configuring access to your private Docker Hub repositories takes three steps:

- Creating an access token for your private Docker Hub repositories
- Getting the user's credentials
- Configuring the credentials in Okteto

## Step 1: Creating an access token for your private Docker Hub repositories

In [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/):

1. Go to **Account Settings > Security**.
2. Click **New Access Token**.
3. Give it a **Name** and `Read-only` **Permissions**.

## Step 2: Getting the user's credentials

When you create the **Access Token**, Docker Hub shows the password to use:

Keep these values: you enter them in Okteto in [Step 3: Configuring the credentials in Okteto](#step-3-configuring-the-credentials-in-okteto).

## Step 3: Configuring the credentials in Okteto

Go to **Admin > Integrations > Registry Credentials**, click **Add Credential**, and enter these values, as [Adding Registry Credentials](admin/registry-credentials/index.mdx#adding-registry-credentials) explains:

- **Type**: `Static`
- **Hostname**: `index.docker.io`
- **Username**: your `username`
- **Password**: your `password`
