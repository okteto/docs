The Admin Dashboard of the Okteto UI is where Admins manage your Okteto instance: its users and Namespaces, the settings that apply to every application environment, and the integrations with other tools. Open it from **Admin** in the main menu of the Okteto UI.

Only users with the Admin role see the Admin Dashboard. The first user who logs in to a new Okteto instance becomes its first Admin. To make another user an Admin, change their role in [Users](admin/users.mdx), or add the `dev.okteto.com/super: "true"` label to their service account in Kubernetes.

The Admin Dashboard has three groups of sections, and each section has a page in these docs.

## Cluster Management

- [Overview](admin/overview.mdx): your license, the versions of Okteto and Kubernetes, and the size of your instance.
- [Installation](admin/installation.mdx): the tasks that complete the setup of your instance.
- [Catalog](admin/catalog.mdx): ready-to-deploy applications for your developers.
- [Users](admin/users.mdx): the accounts of your instance, their roles, and when they were last active.
- [Build Service](admin/build-service.mdx): the performance of the Okteto Build Service and the thresholds that route builds.
- [Nodes](admin/nodes.mdx): the usage of each node of your cluster.
- [Namespaces](admin/namespaces.mdx): every Namespace of your instance, to wake, persist, or transfer them.
- [Previews](admin/previews.mdx): every Preview Environment of your instance.

## Settings

- [Command Line (CLI)](admin/cli.mdx): the default execution mode and the minimum version of the Okteto CLI.
- [Deployments](admin/deployments.mdx): whether redeploying or destroying an application includes its dependencies.
- [Admin Variables](admin/admin-variables.mdx): variables for every application environment of your instance.
- [Garbage Collector](admin/cleanup.mdx): when idle Namespaces and Preview Environments sleep and get deleted.
- [Resource Manager](admin/resource-manager.mdx): the CPU and memory requests of your services, set from their real usage.
- [Known Hosts](admin/ssh-known-hosts.mdx): the SSH hosts that Okteto trusts for Git.

## Integrations

- [General Integrations](admin/general-integrations.mdx): the Okteto SSH key for Git, the Okteto Insights token, the OpenID Connect details, and Okta user deprovisioning.
- [Admin Access Tokens](admin/admin-access-tokens.mdx): tokens with Admin permissions for automations and the Okteto API.
- [Registry Credentials](admin/registry-credentials/index.mdx): access to your private container registries.
- [Cloud Credentials](admin/cloud-credentials/index.mdx): access to your cloud provider for the deploy commands, without static keys.
