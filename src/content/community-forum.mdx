The [Okteto Community forum](https://community.okteto.com/) is where Okteto users and the Okteto team answer questions, troubleshoot issues, and discuss the product. The forum is organized into these categories:

- [How do I](https://community.okteto.com/c/how-do-i/7): how-to questions and answers about configuring and using Okteto.
- [Help](https://community.okteto.com/c/help/6): troubleshooting help when something doesn't work as expected.
- [Feature Requests](https://community.okteto.com/c/feature-requests/8): proposals for new features and improvements to Okteto.
- [Okteto Self-Hosted](https://community.okteto.com/c/okteto-self-hosted/9): installing, upgrading, and operating Okteto Self-Hosted.
